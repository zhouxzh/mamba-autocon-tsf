# Current-Tree Direct ACF Anchor Readout

**Status:** exploratory validation-only evidence. The test split was skipped; this is not a confirmatory or publication result.

## Protocol

The paired arms use the current variable-token `MambaACF` forecast path on ETTh1 and Traffic at H96, with seeds 2021--2023, `MeanStd` window normalization, raw train-only ACF sidecars, and the same optimizer, split, and eight-epoch budget. ACF memory, relation loss, channel gates, horizon calibration, and GDD are disabled. The only registered factor is `mamba_acf_anchor_copy`: the treatment adds a scalar-ReZero residual containing the signed, per-channel maximum-absolute-ACF legal anchor. The anchor is selected from the frozen train-only sidecar and uses causal historical indexing.

The paired mean relative changes are ETTh1 `-0.437%` (3/3 wins), Traffic `-0.304%` (2/3 wins), and pooled `-0.371%` when averaging per-pair relative changes. The ratio of pooled means is `-0.393%` (0.552141 versus 0.554318). A paired t-test over six validation coordinates is descriptive only (`p=0.119` on absolute loss; `p=0.208` on relative change). One Traffic seed regresses by 0.789%, so this is a small, heterogeneous signal rather than a demonstrated improvement.

## Implementation checks

The treatment checkpoint contains a learned `acf_anchor_scale` in every arm. Values are `0.0921--0.1034` on ETTh1 and `0.1193--0.1207` on Traffic, rather than the zero initialization. Thus the branch was instantiated and opened during training. The six treatment sidecars are byte-equivalent to their paired controls within each dataset/seed; only the forecast branch differs. The branch adds one scalar parameter and no channel-by-channel trainable matrix.

The pilot manifest was generated by manually invoking the Mamba environment after the unauthorized draft plan was validated. It records `provenance_id=dry-run` and no source-tree hash, and therefore cannot serve as immutable paper evidence. No test metrics or ACF telemetry were collected.

## Decision

Keep the direct anchor as the only current-tree ACF forecast-level candidate with a non-negligible validation direction. Do not claim accuracy, generalization, or publication readiness from this pilot. The next allowed step is a pre-registered, test-enabled replication with the exact frozen configuration and effective-memory diagnostics; the test results must be reported for both arms and cannot be used to tune the anchor. If that replication does not reproduce the direction across datasets/seeds, stop direct-value-copy variants and redesign the forecast-level coefficient/routing instead of adding more small gates.
